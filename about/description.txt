[h1]Stereo SBS[/h1]
Renders the game in side-by-side stereoscopic 3D for 3D TVs, monitors, projectors and VR headsets that play SBS video.

Open the mod's settings to choose Half SBS (squeezed, what most 3D TVs expect) or Full SBS, and to tune depth strength and convergence.
Experimental. Mouse aiming is not adapted yet, so a controller works best.
Client-side only. It does not change gameplay.
